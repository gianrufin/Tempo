What's inside

Everything a habit tracker forgets to include.

Tempo isn't three apps stitched together — it's one rhythm, with the pieces that usually get left out of "just another habit tracker."

01

Streaks that forgive

Grace days and streak freezes mean one missed morning doesn't erase three weeks of work.

02

Focus timer with auto-DND

Pomodoro, countdown, and stopwatch modes that quietly turn on Do Not Disturb the moment a focus block starts.

03

Tasks with real checklists

Subtasks, priorities, due dates, and one tap to add anything straight to your phone's calendar.

04

Vacation mode

Pause a habit while you travel — it waits for you instead of quietly breaking your streak.

05

Best-time insights

Tempo notices when you actually complete a habit and nudges your reminder toward that time.

06

Yours, backed up

Local or Drive backups, CSV/JSON import, and a home-screen widget — your data never lives only on one phone.
